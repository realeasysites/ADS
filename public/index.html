What We Do

Our Core Services

We handle hazardous material removal and general demolition for residential and commercial properties — from first assessment to final haul-away.

01

Asbestos Abatement

Testing, containment, and removal of asbestos materials following strict Maine DEP guidelines, from floor tile to pipe insulation.

02

Mold Remediation

Identification, treatment, and removal of mold to restore a healthy, safe environment for your home or business.

03

RRP Lead Removal

EPA RRP certified to legally and safely identify, contain, and remove lead-based hazards in older properties.

04

General Demolition

Full and selective teardowns and interior gutting, including complete haul-away and disposal of debris.
